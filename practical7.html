Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about courses, admissions, and departmental policies.

You can register for elective courses via the Student Portal during the official registration window. Log in, go to the Course Registration tab, select your preferred electives, and submit for advisor approval.
Project proposals must be submitted to your assigned faculty advisor by the 4th Friday of the semester. Late submissions require written approval from the Head of Department.
To request transcripts or letters of recommendation, visit the Department Administrative Office (Room 302) or submit an online request through the Student Services Hub on the college website. Processing typically takes 3–5 business days.
Submit your official medical certificate and leave application to the Department Office within 7 days of returning to campus. Ensure you also inform your class coordinator via email.
